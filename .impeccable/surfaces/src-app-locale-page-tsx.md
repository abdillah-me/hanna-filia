# Site-wide visual world and homepage

Scope: every route; the homepage is the first surface. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: teenagers and adults in Batam choosing an English course on a phone; they must believe "I will dare to speak" and send a WhatsApp enquiry for a free trial class. Proof available: class size (8), five sequential levels, the free trial. All testimonials, stats, teachers and photos are sample data until the client supplies real ones.

Chosen direction: the category standard, picked by the owner on the decision page over the dealt directions. Craft bar: EF English First, British Council, Wall Street English, Cakap.

Unresolved: real photography; real contact data; whether prices are shown.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A language-course site that looks exactly like the trusted ones, executed cleanly. It refuses nothing in the category arrangement; it refuses the previous crimson-flooded, ink-bordered Material look.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, warm near-black ink, brand crimson only for actions and small accents, gold only for the "Gratis"/level tag. One humanist sans (Figtree) in two weights. Soft rose-grey tinted bands separate sections. Rounded 8–16px corners, hairline borders, soft offset shadows on hover. Photographs in rounded frames. Dark ink footer.

STORY: I see an English course in Batam for people who want to dare to speak; small classes; five levels I can scan; I can try one class free; I message them on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with logo, links and a filled crimson trial button. Left column: large headline, one supporting sentence, filled crimson primary button and an outlined secondary. Right column: one large rounded classroom photograph. Directly beneath, a row of five equal programme cards.
